Accurate information about disorders of sex development

Disorders of sex development (DSD)*, often referred to as intersex conditions, are congenital conditions in which chromosomal, gonadal, or anatomical sex develops atypically.[1] Each one is a specific medical diagnosis with its own causes, care needs, and lived experience.

* Sometimes referred to as 'differences of sex development.'
