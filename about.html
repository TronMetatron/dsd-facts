About DSD Facts

DSD Facts is an independent educational project. Its goal is to provide accurate, sourced information about disorders of sex development (DSD), to correct misinformation respectfully, and to keep the focus on people who live with these conditions.

Terminology

This site uses disorders of sex development (DSD), the term introduced by the 2006 Consensus Statement.[1] * These conditions are sometimes referred to as 'differences of sex development.'

Editorial standards

  • Every factual claim cites a medical body, peer-reviewed publication, or established patient organization.
  • We correct claims, not people.
  • Where experts disagree, we present each side with sources.
  • Corrections are welcome and will be dated.

Medical disclaimer

This site is for general education only and is not medical advice. It doesn't replace diagnosis or treatment by qualified clinicians. If you or your child may have a DSD, talk to a specialist team (endocrinology, urology/gynecology, genetics, and psychology). In an emergency, such as signs of adrenal crisis in CAH, seek urgent care immediately.
